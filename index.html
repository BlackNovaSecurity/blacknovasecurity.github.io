The cybersecurity risk review

Turn uncertainty
into a plan.

An independent look at your security practices, grounded in how your business actually works.

When your business depends on email, customer data, online systems, and an IT provider, it can be hard to tell where responsibility ends and risk begins.

We review your business priorities, existing safeguards, and available evidence with you and, when appropriate, your IT provider. You leave with clear priorities you can use to make decisions.

How it works

Focused work.
Clear outcomes.

We agree on the scope, fee, and schedule before the engagement begins.

  1. Start with your business

    A brief introductory conversation about your concerns, systems, and goals. We confirm whether the review is the right fit.

  2. Review the evidence

    Interviews and an agreed set of documents and security settings help us assess your current practices and identify gaps.

    We walk through the findings with you, answer questions, and hand over a prioritized roadmap you can put to work.
