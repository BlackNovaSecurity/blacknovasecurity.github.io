The cybersecurity risk review

What does the
review cover?

We look at how your business operates and whether your current security practices support it.

You may already have an IT provider, backups, and security tools in place. A useful review looks at how those pieces work together, where there are gaps, and who is responsible for addressing them.

For example, if your team lost access to email, which work would stop? If someone left the company, who would remove their access? Those questions give us a practical starting point.

I review the agreed documents and settings with you and, where useful, your IT provider. The findings explain what the available evidence supports and where more information is needed.

Ask about a review ↗
WHAT YOU RECEIVE

A summary of the risks

The main findings and how they could affect your business, including interrupted work, access to customer information, and the time needed to recover.

HOW TO PRIORITIZE

The gaps to address first

A prioritized list of issues, the evidence behind them, and why I think they deserve attention. I'll also identify questions we couldn't resolve during the review.

WHAT TO DO NEXT

Recommended next steps

Actions to discuss with your team or IT provider, with suggested ownership and a distinction between near-term work and improvements that need more planning.

Working together

Here's what
to expect.

We agree on the scope, fee, and schedule before starting. That includes what I'll need from you and what you'll receive.

  1. Tell me what's prompting the review

    We'll talk about your business, the systems you depend on, and any concerns you have. If a review fits, I'll propose a scope for you to consider.

  2. Work through the current setup

    I'll ask questions and review the information we've agreed to include. Your IT provider can help explain the existing setup, and I'll follow up on anything that needs clarification.

  3. Discuss the findings and next steps

    We'll walk through the results together so you can ask questions and understand the recommendations. You'll receive a written summary and action roadmap to use with your team.
