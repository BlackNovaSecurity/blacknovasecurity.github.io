Website privacy

Updated October 15, 2026

Optional analytics

When Google Analytics is enabled for this website, we ask before loading it. If you accept, Google processes information about your visit, such as your device and browser, approximate location, referring source, campaign labels, page views, and contact-link clicks. Analytics uses cookies to distinguish browsers; it does not tell us your name.

We use this information to understand how people find the site and which outreach campaigns lead to interest. Our implementation disables advertising signals and does not intentionally send names, email addresses, or recipient identifiers to Google Analytics. Shared campaign labels in website links may identify an outreach campaign or message variation.

You can decline or change your choice through Analytics preferences in the site footer when analytics is enabled. A recognized Global Privacy Control signal prevents our analytics code from loading. We store your preference in your browser. Declining does not affect access to the site.

Email and hosting

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to share through Google Workspace. Clicking an email link on this website opens your email application; it does not submit a message automatically. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process technical request information separately from our optional analytics.
